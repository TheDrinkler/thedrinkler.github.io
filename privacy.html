Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026
Contact: igotdrunkly@gmail.com

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Boozely Drinking App ("Application") for mobile devices that was created by The Drinkler. The Application offers a cocktail menu, advice on what alcohol to mix with which mixer as well as a turn-based drinking virtual card game. The Application is available on the Android Play Store.

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

1User Provided Information

The Application may store the player names and team names you enter in the game setup screen. This information is saved locally on your device solely to restore your last game setup the next time you open the Application. It is never transmitted to us or any third party. You can remove this data at any time by clearing the Application's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

2Automatically Collected Information

The Application detects your device language to set the display language, and stores your last game setup preferences (team names and game mode) locally on your device. None of this information is ever transmitted to us. You can remove this data at any time by clearing the Application's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

3Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

No, this Application does not collect any information about the location of your mobile device.

4Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

No, third parties do not have access to information obtained by the Application.

5What are my opt-out rights?

You may opt out at any time by clearing the Application's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application. Either action will remove all locally stored data (language preference, player names, and game preferences) from your device.

6Data Retention Policy, Managing Your Information

We do not retain any information about you on external servers. The language preference and game setup data (player names, team names, and game preferences) are stored locally on your device solely to improve your experience within the Application. This data remains on your device until you clear the Application's data or uninstall it.

7Children

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. The Application has references to alcohol and alcohol consumption and has as such followed the rules provided by the Store (Android Play Store) to receive the appropriate content rating.

8Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. All data used by the Application (language preference, player names, team names, and game preferences) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.

9Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy here. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly, as continued use of the Application is deemed acceptance of any changes.

10Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to the Application storing your language preference and game setup data (player names, team names, and game preferences) locally on your device to improve your experience. This data is never transmitted to us.
